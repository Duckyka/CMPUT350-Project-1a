Our implementation of the Galaga game followed the guidelines and original source game as closely as we could achieve. We implemented
the galaga game as a finite state machine that managed the gameplay through several stages--waiting on coins, startup, waves, gameplay, and gameover.
As with the original game, we designed our Galaga to be played infinitely until the player eventually loses all their lives during gameplay. Players
first insert coins and start the game before enemies flow onto the screen in waves, falling into formation at the top of the screen. Players then take control
of their ship at the bottom and shoot to destroy the enemies, which in turn fire back and periodically advance to attack the player. There are 3 types of enemies
that have unique attack patterns the player must avoid in order to stay alive. Once all enemies are killed, the player advances to the next level. If the player loses
all of their lives, the game is reset to the main screen, ready to be played again.
